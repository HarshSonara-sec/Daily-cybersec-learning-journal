# SIEM and SOC Analyst Relationship

## SIEM in SOC Operations

**SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)** is a central concept in SOC operations. Let'sDefend's SOC Fundamentals course specifically includes **SIEM and Analyst Relationship** as a core lesson.

## Analyst Perspective

A SOC analyst works with security events and investigation data. SIEM knowledge is therefore connected to the analyst's ability to understand and investigate events rather than treating networking knowledge and SOC work as separate subjects.

## Why Networking Knowledge Matters

Existing networking fundamentals help an analyst interpret security events involving:

- IP addresses
- Ports and protocols
- Client/server communication
- Network connections
- Suspicious traffic patterns

This connects directly with the networking topics already covered in this repository.
