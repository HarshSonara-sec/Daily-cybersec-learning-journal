## Practical Search

```spl
index=security sourcetype=linux_secure host!=mail "Failed password" src_ip=*
| iplocation src_ip
```

Purpose: find failed-password events and enrich their source IP addresses with geographic information.

## Geographic Analysis

```spl
index=security sourcetype=linux_secure host!=mail "Failed password" src_ip=*
| iplocation src_ip
| geostats count by Country
```

Purpose: summarize failed-password activity geographically for visualization.

## Key Takeaway

