### 3. SOC Levels
Reviewed the common SOC tier structure:

- **Tier 1:** Monitoring, alert review, initial triage, basic investigation, and escalation.
- **Tier 2:** Deeper investigation, incident handling, correlation, and response support.
- **Tier 3:** Advanced analysis, threat hunting, malware analysis, and detection engineering.
- **Tier 4:** Advanced threat research/security research in organizations that use a fourth tier.

> Tier structures vary between organizations.
