# Let'sDefend — SOC Fundamentals

**Completed:** 2026-09-23  
**Platform:** Let'sDefend  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Badge:** SOC Member

## Course Purpose

SOC Fundamentals introduces how a Security Operations Center (SOC) works and the tools used for security investigation. The course is positioned as a starting point for people building a SOC/Blue Team career.

## Course Topics

1. Introduction to SOC
2. SOC Types and Roles
3. SOC Analyst and Their Responsibilities
4. SIEM and Analyst Relationship
5. Log Management
6. EDR — Endpoint Detection and Response
7. SOAR — Security Orchestration, Automation and Response
8. Threat Intelligence Feed
9. Common Mistakes Made by SOC Analysts

## Expected Outcomes

- Understand the SOC and the role of a SOC analyst.
- Build practical understanding of log management, EDR and SOAR.
- Understand security incident analysis and response at a foundational level.
- Develop a proactive mindset for security operations.

## Connection to My Existing Studies

- **Networking:** network protocols and traffic knowledge support SOC investigation.
- **Linux:** system/process/service knowledge supports endpoint and log investigation.
- **Forensics:** investigation concepts connect to evidence and endpoint analysis.
- **Web Security:** web attack knowledge can help interpret security alerts.
- **Nmap / Wireshark:** existing reconnaissance and packet-analysis knowledge can support future SOC investigations.

## Learning Note

This course reinforced existing cybersecurity fundamentals and introduced the operational relationship between analysts, logs and security tools. Future SOC study will move from fundamentals into SIEM, detection, alert triage, incident response, threat hunting and hands-on investigation.
